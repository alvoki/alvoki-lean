The shift versions call a separate four-word row helper three times in the
compiled alternating function (two loop sites and the odd tail). Clang inlines
it with memcpy, which exposes the three sets of four ownership checks.
The added assumption lets Clang remove one at each of those three sites.
It also removes one check in packed repack with memcpy and one in owned
encode with either load/store implementation. Dense retains its one site.
Each loop still carries an ownership-check slow path per iteration.
